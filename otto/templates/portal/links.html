For agents

For clients

Links and numbers you send to a client: a change-of-address form, a claims page, a payment portal. Group them by company so they are easy to find while you are on the phone.

How it is organised

Every entry has a section (the company or topic), a title and an address. Lists sort by section, then title. Press C on a highlighted row, or use Copy, to put the name and address on your clipboard ready to paste into a text or email.
